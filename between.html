between

An AI-driven wearable and app that helps long-distance relationships stay emotionally present through haptics, mood sharing, and gentle daily rituals.

Skills
UX Design, Concept Design, Wireframing, Prototyping
Tools
Figma, FigJam
Timeline
Spring 2025

Overview

Between is an AI-driven wearable watch and app system that helps long-distance couples, friends, and families stay emotionally connected — using haptic touch, mood sharing, and daily emotional rituals without pressure or noise. For the relationships that matter most, this is emotional presence without pressure: touch, reflect, and grow closer, one small moment at a time.

Problem

Long-distance relationships often suffer from a lack of shared presence and non-verbal emotional communication.

Whether romantic, familial, or platonic, the distance between people who care about each other shows up in the small things — a missed call, a feeling that didn't fit in a text, a ritual that quietly disappeared. Three patterns surfaced again and again.

Asynchronous schedules and life rhythms.

Friends, family, or partners in different time zones or with busy lives often miss each other.

Overstimulated tech environments.

Most devices demand attention and contribute to digital fatigue.

Shallow digital interactions.

Social media and messaging apps often prioritize speed and visibility over emotional depth.

Archetype

Nathan, 26 — thoughtful, routine-oriented, and recently long distance.

Nathan and his girlfriend Lily recently became long distance after dating in person for three years. He's proud of her for chasing her dream job, but often misses the quiet intimacy of being together. He's not great at texting constantly, but wants to feel close to her in little ways that matter.

Pain points

  • With being in different time zones, it makes it hard for Nathan to find the "right moment" to talk with Lily.
  • He sometimes struggles to express what he is feeling in a text message.
  • He misses the little rituals between the two of them — hugging, messaging her feet, hearing her laugh during dinner.

Solution

Promote self-awareness through mood setting, gentle check-ins, and symbolic rituals.

Between is designed to encourage emotional vocabulary and reflection — turning each of the three problem areas into a gentler, more intentional pattern of connection.

Syncing schedules and life rhythms.

Prompts and emotional updates are asynchronous. Each person can respond in their own time, yet feel heard and emotionally seen.

Calming down tech environments.

Designed as a calm technology that blends into daily life, enhancing presence without overwhelming attention.

Making digital interactions meaningful.

Encourages meaningful microinteractions that are personal, not performative.

✦ Concept ✦

between

For the relationships that matter most, this is emotional presence without pressure. Touch, reflect, and grow closer. One small moment at a time.

Watch

A quiet wearable that surfaces presence, not notifications.

The watch shows your partner's mood and current status — "Lily is on her lunch break, well-rested, caffeinated, peaceful" — and lets you respond with a haptic touch or a quick voice memo. It's designed to fade into daily life, offering presence without demanding attention.

Home

A home screen built around emotional context, not notifications.

The app's home centers on four gentle signals — what your partner is doing, what you want to send them, what to reflect on, and how they've been feeling lately.

Partner widget.

Shows your partner's current mood and status at a glance.

Send a drift.

Pictures or memos drift quietly until your partner is ready to view them — no pressure to respond.

Echo of the day.

Gentle prompts arrive randomly throughout the day, inviting reflection without demanding it.

Weather of their heart.

An emotional forecast based on your partner's recent moods, visualized as a soft landscape.

Rituals

Four small rituals that grow into something shared.

Drifts arrive without urgency, echoes invite reflection rather than reaction, and the memory garden becomes a soft archive of the small moments that hold a relationship together.

  1. 01

    Picture drift

    Snap a quiet moment. Send it. It drifts in the background of their day until they're ready to look.

  2. 02

    Memo drift

    Record a short voice memo — a laugh, a thought, a goodnight — and let it wait for them.

  3. 03

    Echo

    Answer a shared prompt with a few sentences or a voice note. Reflection over reaction.

  4. 04

    Memory garden

    Drifts and echoes settle into a shared garden that grows over time — a soft archive of small moments.

Final Screens

A wearable and an app that hold space for each other's presence.

The final screens bring the watch, home, drifts, echoes, and the memory garden into a single calm system — designed to sit quietly in the background of daily life until something small and meaningful is worth surfacing.

Reflection

Designing for emotional presence taught me how loud most apps really are.

Between asked me to design against the instinct to add — more notifications, more feeds, more reactions. The hardest part wasn't inventing new features, it was deciding what to leave out so the experience could feel calm. Drifts that wait, echoes that don't demand a response, a watch that whispers instead of buzzes.

The project also reframed how I think about emotional design. A simple mood tag or a shared garden carries more weight than a long message when the underlying intent is to feel close, not to communicate efficiently. Restraint became the design language.

What worked

Anchoring the product in Nathan's archetype kept every feature decision tied to a real emotional need rather than a generic use case.

What I'd change

Prototyping the haptic side of the watch sooner — paper and Figma can only carry a calm-technology concept so far.

What's next

Exploring how Between could extend beyond romantic partners — to family, close friends, and other relationships where presence matters more than frequency.
