Brief

Creative Juices is a laid-back networking and social event where PSUGD students and the broader creative community can unwind, connect, and spark inspiration over complimentary drinks and food.

Think of it as happy hour meets creative recharge — a space to exchange ideas, build relationships, and share inspiration in a casual yet stimulating setting.
