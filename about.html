Work About Play

Hello there, I'm Olivia! I'm a Vietnamese-American designer with a focus in UX. I have a passion for blending functionality and creativity to create experiences that feel both intuitive and emotionally engaging. Alongside UX, I also love exploring branding, illustration, storytelling, and interactive design.

A lot of my work is inspired by themes of identity and human connection, combining digital experiences with expressive visuals and experimentation. I enjoy exploring programming and interactive media as tools to bring ideas to life in more dynamic ways.

When I'm not glued to my electronics and designing, I'm out and about, indulging in nature or at the Oregon Zoo. If I'm not out, I'm baking in the comfort of my home.
